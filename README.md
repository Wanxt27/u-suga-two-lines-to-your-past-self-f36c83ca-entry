> Don't send that 2 a.m. text to Maya from the kitchen table — put the phone down and go to bed.
> That one unsent message is why she still picks up when you call, and why you now sleep on every word you can't take back.

Line one warns about one specific moment you can picture — 2 a.m., the kitchen table, a text to Maya — and line two traces the cause and effect: the unsent message is why she still answers, and why the writer now sleeps on every word that can't be taken back. Plain voice, no wasted words, one decision with a real consequence.
